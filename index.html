ABOUT J&G

Simple food, satisfying flavor, made for the community.

J&G Chicken Pastil is a local food business serving an easy, affordable, and flavorful take on the Filipino favorite Chicken Pastil. Our goal is simple: give customers a filling meal they can enjoy anytime—whether they are heading to school, work, or just craving comfort food.

Aside from our rice meals, we also offer Chicken Pastil in a Jar for customers who want the same savory flavor ready at home.
